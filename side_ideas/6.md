# Side Idea 6

## Real-World Masked Market Reconstruction

Core framing:

- treat Polymarket as a dynamic system of related probabilistic variables rather than a set of isolated markets;
- hide one target market `A` while keeping the rest of the market system visible;
- ask the model to reconstruct the hidden market from the surrounding market state.

Core benchmark:

- given the contemporaneous market universe with `A` hidden, predict `outcome(A)`.

Stronger extension:

- given the rest of the market system, reconstruct the probability trajectory of `A`.

Why it is interesting:

- it turns Polymarket into a real-world masked modeling problem;
- it is closer to a foundation-model benchmark than a standard tabular forecasting task;
- it naturally supports multimodal context: text, metadata, time series, weak graph structure, and external signals.

Why it may fit NeurIPS:

- it frames the problem as conditional reconstruction in a dynamic structured system;
- it supports pretraining-style objectives and downstream probes;
- it can be evaluated in held-out, cold-start, and transfer settings.

Main risk:

- Polymarket contains many near-duplicate or closely related markets, so duplicate control and group-based splits are essential.

Careful first version:

- use held-out market outcome inference as the core benchmark;
- keep full trajectory reconstruction as a harder extension rather than the only main task.

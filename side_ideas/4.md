## Compact External-Context State for Information Uptake and Risk Decomposition

Learn a compact representation of external and cross-market context that explains how prediction markets absorb, delay, or decompose information into event-specific beliefs.

Two scientific readings of the same setup:

1. Delayed information uptake:
   more liquid external markets move first, and slower event markets update later.

2. Risk decomposition:
   if event markets are already close to efficient, then their moves can be interpreted as a decomposition of common external volatility into specific political or economic risks.

Practical formulation:

- input:
  target market `A`,
  related markets `B`,
  external signals `E`

- model:
  learn a compact context state `z_context`

- evaluate:
  does `z_context` improve forecasting, predict repricing, and explain variation across related markets?

The key framing is not "can we trade this?" but:

Can we learn a compact context representation that captures delayed information uptake and event-specific risk translation in prediction markets?

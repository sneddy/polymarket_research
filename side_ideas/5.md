## External Shock Propagation into Prediction Markets

Detect structural breaks in external liquid markets such as `BTC` or `ETH`, then measure which Polymarket markets update, how strongly, and how quickly.

Basic setup:

1. Identify external shock windows, for example `|return| > 2σ`.
2. Look at the next `Δh` window in Polymarket.
3. Measure which markets reprice and by how much.

Core questions:

- Which markets are shock-sensitive?
- How fast does the shock propagate into event markets?
- Do related markets move coherently or selectively?

Two scientific readings:

1. Delayed information uptake:
   liquid external markets move first and event markets incorporate that information later.

2. Risk decomposition:
   a common external volatility shock gets translated into specific political, economic, or thematic risks across different markets.

Possible model direction:

- input:
  external shock features,
  current Polymarket state,
  target market metadata

- output:
  probability and magnitude of repricing for each market

This is not mainly a trading question. The stronger framing is:

How do external shocks propagate into a dynamic system of event probabilities?
